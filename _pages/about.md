I am a PhD researcher in Generative AI at the [BMW Group](https://www.bmwgroup.com/) and part of the Automated Software Testing group of [Andrea Stocco](https://tsigalko18.github.io) at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en/). Before that, I was a research and development engineer at [fortiss](https://www.fortiss.org/en/), working on testing and assurance of autonomous systems.

My research focuses on making AI-enabled systems reliable and safe through automated testing.

Current research themes:

* **Testing LLM-based applications** :speech_balloon: — search-based testing of LLM apps, vision-language models, and in-car conversational assistants [ICSE 2027, SANER 2026, ASE 2026, JSS 2026, ITSC 2026]
* **Simulation-based testing of automated driving systems** :oncoming_automobile: — multi-fidelity testing and simulator ensembles [ICSE 2027, EMSE 2026]
